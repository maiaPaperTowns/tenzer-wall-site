# Original-photo prototype — November 22, 2026

The active build uses the three exact user-supplied JPEGs, not the generated ink-wash replacement landscapes. Original composition and color are retained with a very faint procedural paper texture. The full photographs are contained on wide displays, with softened same-photo surroundings for River and Fish rather than cropping away their subjects.

- Moon: original lunar texture and final placement on its black background; rises in **6 seconds**, reduced from the generated version's 28-second rise. No invented mountains, lake, clouds or stars.
- River: water-only two-phase texture flow, with conservative bank/rock exclusions. Trees and scenery remain still.
- Fish: bounded school deformation, subtle water refraction, drifting light and motes. This is photo-based deformation, not individually segmented 3D fish.
- Reduced motion: still photograph; no rise, flow or swimming.

The original interface, random falling characters, single tap, Sound/About controls and context-menu suppression remain. Generated assets are retained as inactive history. The previous generated-prototype document is historical and no longer describes the active renderer.

The originals are 724 pixels wide: responsive rendering supports the 7680×2160 wall layout, but cannot supply native 8K photographic detail. Full-resolution licensed originals would improve sharpness. Actual infrared-touch/hardware testing remains necessary.
