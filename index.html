Welcome to the Kanji Wall

Welcome to the Kanji Wall, an immersive artistic experience that connects Kanji characters to their meanings. To explore, click on a falling Kanji character to see it transformed into its meaning. Enjoy!

About the Kanji Wall

This immersive Kanji Wall was designed to provide an artistic experience to help people connect Kanji characters to their meaning. The experience was designed by Dave Berque, Hiroko Chiba and Maia Le and was implemented by Maia Le. The experience is modeled after a museum exhibit produced by a Japanese design group called teamLab. At DePauw, the project was supported by the Tenzer Technology Center and the Information Technology Associates (ITAP) program.
