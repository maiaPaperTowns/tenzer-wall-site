## First six clusters

Use case: compositing. Asset: transparent botanical cluster atlas for animated blooms, six distinct clusters arranged in a strict 3 columns by 2 rows grid on one 1536x1024 landscape canvas, equal cells, generous transparent gutters, no cluster crossing cell boundaries. Use supplied azalea, pink rose, pink flower clusters and botanical plates as botanical reference material, keeping recognizable flower shapes and colors. Each cell contains a graceful organic cluster of 3 to 6 blossoms with a few fine curved stems and soft small leaves, mixed blossom sizes, asymmetrical airy composition, no bouquet wrappers or vases. Cell 1: large pink azalea cluster in translucent watercolor; cell 2: blush wild roses and small white blossoms in delicate botanical illustration; cell 3: blue violets with a few pale pink blossoms in soft gouache; cell 4: yellow narcissus and cream flowers in botanical watercolor; cell 5: small rose-pink clustered flowers with graceful greenery in painterly brushwork; cell 6: airy mixed white and blush wild roses in luminous soft watercolor. Harmonized muted natural palette, refined museum-quality detail, feather-light edges but crisp petal structure. Actual transparent background and transparent space between stems and petals. No colored backdrop, paper texture background, shadows, text, labels, grid lines or borders. Distinct large and small blossoms within each cluster, beautiful soft natural silhouettes.

Saved as `assets/derived/flower-clusters-v3.png`.

## Six additional mixed clusters

Use case: compositing. Create a second transparent botanical cluster atlas for an interactive flower wall. Six distinct mixed-flower arrangements in a strict 3-column 2-row grid, each confined to its equal cell with 5 percent transparent padding; overall landscape 1536x1024. Reference the supplied azaleas, roses, clustered pink flowers, yellow narcissus and blue violet botanical plates. CRITICAL each cluster MIXES different flower species/colors, never six uniform single-species bouquets. Strongly varied asymmetrical silhouettes: top left wide curved branch of pink azalea, tiny white roses and blue violets; top center sparse tall spray of yellow narcissus, blush wild rose and delicate blue flowers; top right dense crescent of large white wild roses, crimson azalea and tiny pink clustered blossoms; bottom left airy trailing branch with small lavender flowers, one yellow bloom and pale pink roses; bottom middle compact irregular burst mixing 2 large pink roses, tiny white blossoms, yellow narcissus and violet accents; bottom right long diagonal S-shaped arrangement of crimson azalea, ivory roses, blue violets and sparse fine green leaves. Vary cluster density, bloom scale, stem length, leaf size, and negative space. Organic stems, realistic botanical petals, soft museum-quality watercolor/gouache harmonized across cells. No circular wreaths, no repeated mirrored layouts, no florist wrapping, no vase, no text, no boxes. ACTUAL transparent alpha around clusters and between stems, no paper or colored background, no shadows.

Saved as `assets/derived/flower-mixed-v4.png`. The renderer mixes these with the existing single blooms and varies size, spacing, orientation and reveal time.
